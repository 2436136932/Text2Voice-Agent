feat: expose the audio-enhance endpoint in the UI (was backend-only)

POST /media/enhance worked and was tested, but had no frontend caller, so
the feature was unreachable in the app. The reference-clip trim panel now
has a 优化音色 button next to 应用裁剪:

- calls POST /media/enhance, swaps the picker to the returned (new) media
  id, and rebinds the trim selection to the shortened clip;
- surfaces backend warnings ("still under 1 second after trimming") as a
  toast instead of a silent success;
- keeps the original available through the existing 恢复原文件 button.

Also verified the whole chain against real speech (not synthetic tones):
upload 94.3s video -> trim 12s -> enhance -> clone-generate succeeded,
audio 353KB/7.36s, degraded=false. The ffmpeg chain was validated
separately: on a real clip padded with 2s of silence at each end, the
enhance step removed 3.70s of padding and normalized peak 32768 -> 26151
(-16 LUFS). Byte-identical output on a clip with no leading/trailing
silence is expected, not a bug.

Adds scripts/e2e_enhance_clone.py for future full-chain checks.
